# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in TechConnect a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions, feedback, and different viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.
- Communicate professionally in issues, discussions, and pull requests.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or project collaboration.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact or reporting channels. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
